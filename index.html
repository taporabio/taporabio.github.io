Put it online, free

Pick one. Each takes about five minutes. Then paste your address into the website field of your Instagram, TikTok, X and LinkedIn profiles.

GitHub Pages
  1. Make a free account at github.com, then press New repository.
  2. Name it yourusername.github.io, using your real GitHub username. Keep it Public and press Create repository.
  3. Press "uploading an existing file". Unzip your download, drag the folder in, and press Commit changes.
  4. Open Settings, then Pages. Under Source choose Deploy from a branch, pick main and / (root), then Save.
  5. After a minute or two your page is live at https://yourusername.github.io/yourname/
Netlify Drop (easiest)
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
  2. Unzip your download and drag the folder onto the page.
  3. Open Site configuration, then Change site name, and type your link name. Your address becomes yourname.netlify.app
Cloudflare Pages
  1. In the Cloudflare dashboard open Workers and Pages, then Create, then Pages, then Upload assets.
  2. Name the project with your link name and upload the unzipped folder.
  3. Press Deploy. Your address is yourname.pages.dev
Hosting pages for other people

To give friends or clients their own links under your domain, keep one GitHub repository with one folder per person, so each page opens at yourdomain.com/theirname. Each person sends you their ZIP and you add the folder. Doing this automatically, with sign up and instant links, needs a small server and logins. That is a bigger build and is not part of this file.

If a name is already taken, add a number or your city to it.
